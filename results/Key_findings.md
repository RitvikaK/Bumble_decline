# Key Findings

## 1. Account issues eroded platform trust

Account issues generated the highest negative sentiment among high-volume categories.

Key drivers:
- Login & verification failures
- Unjustified bans/suspensions
- Account access failures

---

## 2. Premium monetisation reduced perceived value

Forced subscription was the largest complaint category.

Users frequently associated monetisation with:
- Frustration
- Poor value for money
- Perceived monetisation pressure

---

## 3. Match quality weakened Bumble's core proposition

Match quality became an increasingly significant complaint area.

Key drivers:
- No matches
- Bad quality matches
- Low-effort interactions
